Kionan

Population: 2.5 million
Cities: Grine, Wraka, Zhane, Flou, Iogay, Shofta.
Language: Izen but everyone can write Faden because of school.

Kionan’s main trade is furniture, such as beds and Ice boxes and ovens. They by materials from Dlandia and Follow blueprints from the mainland. Kionanies are extremely proud of their craft. Another of their trades are tourism- they have stunning desserts and offer a warm environment, to relax in. Since Carpenters are highly respected and they are not overly affected by Sawyers taxing.Sawyer almost favours Kionanies over high kingdomers as they made him the most amazing bed ever. So he makes sure that their main trade comes free of taxes. They have the gears and light of woodlandia whilst also trading with the main land for energy. Their land may seem like a paradise but Kionan’s ministers are strict on the law and if they can’t give you to the lost they’ll send you to Lizak which puts a lot of people off from applying to live there. They also only let people with flawless school records join their society which can be rather closed minded. They also have vast deserts with glorious oasis.
