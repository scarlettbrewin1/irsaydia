Dlandia

Population: 10 million
Cities: Asterbridge, Xiyphis, Zlerough, Trenburt.
Language: Izen but everyone can write Faden because of school.

Dlandia’s main trade is wood. They have large pine forests so they cut down trees at a moderate pace and sell them to the rest of the world. Another big trade for them is honey. Trenburt is the perfect place for pine to grow so most woodcutters live around that area. People with other jobs live in larger cities around the country and focus on contributing to the country. Dlandians have a more strategic solution to the lack of technology they use gears to move stuff with ease and specially designed candles work by burning wax mixed with turen fungi which causes a bright light to burst from it. There is a reasonably high distinction between the economic classes in Dlandia ans they are more about work to live and live to work so hopefully you don’t hate your job. Most Dlandians are great at hunting and gathering or have a business based mindset, that leads them to be extremely strategic and knowledgeable..
