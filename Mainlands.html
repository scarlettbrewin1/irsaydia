Mainlands

Population: 30 million
Mainland is made up of 6 cities all names after the sacred animals Cowein, Foxizen, Frogtonia, Icrania, Kragno’s end, and Catzenburg.
The main language there is Faden but most Mainlanders can write fluently in Izen as it is taught at school.

Mainland is the heart of the planet. It is the centre of innovation, out of school education, art and trade. But as the gods demand more taxes on faide there they need to create more art and they can no longer function financially the way they did so the society is becoming more and more capitalist based and more and more people are sinking into poverty but many people are afraid they’ll anger the gods by leaving. Most of their power comes from the prisoners on The Land Of The Lost but most mainlanders don’t realise this though the ones they do don’t care or find ways to moralise it. Since faide can’t absorb others energy that is still apart of them they found a way to make it into balls of energy still in that form that slowly releases heat/light energy this energy is used in light and some household appliances..
