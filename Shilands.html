Shiland

Population: 80,000
Cities: Primary, tertiary, secondary.
Language: Izen and Faden

Shiland is the region which everyone aged 8-20 is sent to learn. They have three month long breaks every year. One at the start, one at the end and one in the middle, otherwise they and the teachers are permanent residents of the island. Shiland is made up of three islands students aged 8-12 live on the primary island, students aged 12-18 liv on the secondary island and students aged 18-20 live on the tertiary island. Most supplies are shipped to the island but for tertiary and secondary students to get extra stuff beyond the basics they must pick up jobs like cleaning for the school or helping to run a cash which is meant to teach them work and entrepreneurial skills in low stakes conditions to prepare them for the real world. School runs 5 days a week for 7 hours a day in primary, five hours in secondary and three hours in tertiary since tertiary students are expected to be able to control their own studies as they prepare to enter the workforce. During the school year they hold balls and festivals on the beautiful beaches for the students to attend.
