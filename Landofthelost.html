Population: 500,000
There are two main prisons on the land of the lost. One is for general rule breakers and the other one is for the snapped. They claim they give prisoners enough but they only give them just enough. Average prisoners are kept in a labyrinth with the heat from the glass roof their only source of energy. For those who have snapped are trapped in an even smaller dark room and since they can now create their own energy and are practically immortal they stay alive that way. All prisoners have a chip in their neck that allows them to be trapped and their energy to be harvested and used by average people. Usually mainlanders. They are the safest most efficient source of energy for faide.People are imprisoned depending on how much harm they cause other people is their sentence is determined by a governor. If a governor is to sentence someone unfairly they get sent to the land of the lost themself for a full year. Every time someone goes to the land of the lost they get lost tattooed on their forehead and will then be referred to as lost for the rest of their lives. So many hide for their lives even once out of prison.
